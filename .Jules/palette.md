## 2026-10-08 - Refactored Article Cards for TalkBack
**Learning:** In dynamically constructed Android layouts using native views, screen readers like TalkBack read each child view separately. In complex list items like news cards with many small metadata text views (time, source, title, summary), this forces users to swipe excessively and degrades the experience. Applying `View.IMPORTANT_FOR_ACCESSIBILITY_NO` to non-actionable children and placing aggregated `contentDescription` and the click listener on the parent container produces optimally sized touch targets and a cohesive reading experience.
**Action:** When creating dynamic UI cards or list items, apply click listeners and aggregated content descriptions to the parent container, and set `View.IMPORTANT_FOR_ACCESSIBILITY_NO` on individual non-interactive child elements.
